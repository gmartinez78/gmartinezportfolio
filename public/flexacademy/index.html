Questions

Before you choose a path.

Is this another guru course?

No. The programmes are built by the founders of The Flex and Base360, from the systems they use in an active rental operation.

Which offer should I choose?

Choose Launch if you are starting your first STR business. Choose Scale if you already operate units and need help growing into a more organised company.

Who are the programmes for?

They are designed for ambitious English-speaking founders across the UK, US, Canada, Australia and other markets.

What does Scale include?

Scale is a 12-week programme with weekly group sessions, a monthly 1:1 with a founder, operator community, SOPs and Base360 access.

What if I am not ready to join?

Get the free STR scaling checklist or join the waitlist. You can return when the timing is right.
